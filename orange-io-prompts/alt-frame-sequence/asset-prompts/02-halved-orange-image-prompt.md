# Asset 2: The halved orange (scene 3)

Video generators handle cutting fruit badly, so the halving is done in code with two transparent images. Paste this into an AI image generator.

## Prompt

```
Top-down studio photo of a ripe orange cut cleanly in half, the two halves
lying face-up side by side with a small gap, vivid glistening segments, a few
juice droplets, soft warm light, plain pure white background, photorealistic,
high detail.
```

## What to do with the result

1. Cut the background away to get transparent PNGs (see `03-process-assets.md`).
2. Split the image into two files: `orange-half-left.png` and `orange-half-right.png`. Crop each tightly, and give both the same height so they line up when they sit together.
3. Also make `orange-whole.png`: a cutout of the orange from the last frame of the orchard video.

## Tips

- If the halves come out at different sizes or angles, generate again or crop and scale them to match. They should look like the two halves of one orange when placed next to each other.
- A plain white background gives the cleanest cutout.
