# Asset 1: The orchard video (scenes 1 and 2)

Paste this into an AI video generator (Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway, or similar). It is one continuous take, so the page can scrub it with scroll. This is not a Claude Code prompt, so it lives outside the `prompts/` folder.

## Prompt (16:9, about 8 to 12 seconds)

```
Single continuous shot, no cuts. Photorealistic, golden hour, sunlit orchard.
Opens wide: a man in his 30s (short dark hair, white linen shirt, sleeves
rolled) and a woman in her 30s (long dark hair tied back, olive-green linen
dress) water a lush orange tree heavy with ripe fruit; he pours from a
weathered copper watering can, the water sparkling in warm backlight. The
camera pushes in slowly and steadily toward one ripe orange near the center of
the tree. The woman's hand enters frame, cups the orange and twists it free,
then lifts it toward the camera until it is centered and fills about a third
of the frame against a softly blurred green-and-amber background, and holds
still for one second. Smooth steady camera, no shake, no cuts, 24fps, 16:9,
shallow depth of field, soft film grain.
```


## Mobile version

Generate the same prompt again at 9:16 and use that for the mobile frames. Keep the orange centered in the last frame in both versions, because the whole-orange cutout is positioned over it.

## Tips

- Keep the camera slow and steady with no fast cuts. A smooth push-in scrubs best.
- The last frame must show the orange held still and centered. That frame becomes the source of the whole-orange cutout, so its lighting matches.
- Re-roll until the orange keeps its shape and color the whole way through.
